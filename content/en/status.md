## What exists today

- A [draft specification](/spec/0.1/) (SANAD-SPEC-0.1): data model, canonical hashing, the recognition function (rules R1 to R8), transparency log, privacy and discovery, threats, governance and open questions.
- This website, in English with draft translations.
- A reference implementation with 20 conformance vectors, and an interactive explainer hosted by the Kamangir project for now.

## What has not been done

- Review by a standards body, a lawyer or an external cryptographer.
- Trademark registration (the steward will file the application himself; not yet filed) and copyright deposit (see [the name](/name-and-ip/) and [intellectual property policy](/ipr/)).
- Independent implementations, conformance testing, real attestors outside the author's circle.
- Native-speaker review of the German, Arabic and Persian texts.

## What Sanad does not claim

- It does not **accredit** institutions or certify people. Verifiers decide what to accept.
- It does not prove **honesty**: a person can still be helped outside the camera. A chain says what was observed.
- It does not replace **legal recognition**: degrees remain what states and institutions say they are.
- It is not a **blockchain** and has no **token**. An optional public timestamp of log checkpoints may use an existing service.

## Who is responsible

Dr. Arash Kermani Kolankeh is the author and interim steward. A foundation is to be formed in the United Arab Emirates. The free-zone foundation regimes examined (ADGM, DIFC) require a council of at least two, so a second steward is being appointed; a lawyer must confirm other forms.
