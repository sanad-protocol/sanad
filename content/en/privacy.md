## What this website does itself

The pages are static. They set **no cookies**, use **no analytics, advertising or tracking**, load **no fonts, scripts or embedded content from other servers** and contain **no forms**. A content security policy in each page enforces this. The operator therefore processes no personal data of visitors through the site itself.

## Hosting and server logs (GitHub Pages)

The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (USA) on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub's privacy statement, GitHub may collect technical information from visitors to a GitHub Pages site, including logs of visitor IP addresses, to maintain the security and integrity of the site and service. GitHub is an independent controller for these logs and the operator does not receive or use them. Purpose and legal basis: security and integrity of the service, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests) on the part of GitHub and of the operator in a secure site. Retention is set by GitHub; see its privacy statement. Source: [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Transfer to the United States

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Article 15), rectification (16), erasure (17), restriction (18), notification (19), data portability (20) and **objection** (21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (77). Write to contact@sanadprotocol.org. The operator lives in the United Arab Emirates; the competent supervisory authority is to be named by the steward. Visitors from the European Union or the United Kingdom may also contact the authority of their own country. The Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data of the United Arab Emirates may also apply.
