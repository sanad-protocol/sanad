## Contents of version 0.1

1. In one page: the idea and the non-goals.
2. Principles and terms.
3. The data model: skill, claim, evidence, attestation, policy.
4. Canonical form, hashes and signatures.
5. The recognition function (rules R1 to R8) and its result.
6. Carrying a Sanad bundle in a verifiable credential.
7. The skill graph and inference of hidden skills.
8. The transparency log and optional timestamping.
9. Attestors and trust.
10. Privacy, discovery and matching.
11. Conformance and test vectors.
12. Threats and mitigations.
13. Governance and evolution; open questions.
