## Sanad is not a blockchain

It uses hashes, signatures and an append-only log, which are older techniques. It has no token and no mining. A checkpoint of the log may optionally be timestamped with an existing public service.

## Sanad is not accreditation

Sanad does not accredit institutions or certify people. It practises decentralised assessment: attestors sign what they observed, and each verifier decides what to accept under its own policy.

## Responsibility for the project

The author, Dr. Arash Kermani Kolankeh, is interim steward; a second steward is being appointed and a foundation in the United Arab Emirates is in preparation. The [governance charter](/governance/) provides for at least three independent stewards from two regions before version 1.0, or by 31 December 2028.

## Costs

The specification, the code and verification are free. A live sitting may involve a fee for the assessors, set by the organiser of the sitting; the protocol does not require one. The project has received no funding; see [Support](/support/).

## Limits of what a chain proves

A chain records what was observed and by whom. It does not prove that nobody was helped outside the observation. Procedures and observers reduce this risk; software cannot remove it.

## Inferred skills

Skills that follow from a demonstrated skill through the prerequisite graph are marked inferred, with their derivation. They are shown to the holder first and published only at the holder's choice.

## No ranking of persons

The specification forbids a score of a person across skills. An index may return the set of persons who satisfy a query and have consented to be listed; contact is mediated.

## Privacy

The holder controls what is published, using selective disclosure. There is no central database of profiles. The log contains hashes only. Employment matching can be regulated as high-risk in the European Union and requires human oversight and legal review before it is offered.

## Continuity

A sealed instruction names a stand-by person to transfer the domain, the repositories and the keys. The sunset clause of the charter limits dependence on the founder.
