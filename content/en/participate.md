<!-- glance -->
- Everyone may comment, implement and attest. No membership is needed.
- Discussion is public. Decisions are written down with reasons.
- A W3C Community Group is planned for the standardisation work.
<!-- /glance -->

## Comment on the specification

Open an issue in the project repository (to be published at `sanad-protocol/sanad`) or write to contact@sanadprotocol.org. A comment period of 14 days applies before any decision. Please say which section and which rule you mean, and what you would change.

## Implement it

Build a verifier, an issuer, an attestor, a log or an indexer. Run the public vectors, publish your results and tell the project. A second and a third independent implementation are the most valuable contributions at this stage.

## Become an attestor

Anyone may attest by publishing a decentralised identifier and signing. Nobody vets attestors for the protocol: each verifier chooses by policy. Independence is not enforced by the protocol: it rests on declared conflicts of interest, blind marking and the verifier's policy (for example, attestors from different institutions). Declare every conflict of interest.

## Contribute

Contributions are signed off under the Developer Certificate of Origin (a line "Signed-off-by: Name <email>" in each commit). The code of conduct is the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1; reports go to contact@sanadprotocol.org.

## Standardisation

The planned route is a public draft, a reference implementation, a conformance suite, at least three independent implementers, then a W3C Community Group (creation is free and needs five supporters) and later a Working Group or an equivalent. Sanad does not declare itself a standard; adoption does.

## Security

Report vulnerabilities in the specification or in reference code to security@sanadprotocol.org. See the [security page](/security/) for scope, response times and the measures of this website.
