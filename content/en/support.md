## What a grant would add

A grant would add speed and independence, not the design, which is open and free. Qualitative only; no amounts are claimed.

- Time of an editor and of a security reviewer for the specification.
- A paid independent review of the cryptographic parts and of the accessibility of the site.
- Legal advice for the foundation, the trademark and data protection, instead of waiting for a volunteer.
- Native-language review of translations.
- A second implementation in another language, written by an independent developer.
- Costs of a first pilot with real assessors.

## Twelve-month plan

Planning estimate, to be confirmed by the steward. Dates are targets, not promises; see also the [roadmap](/roadmap/).

| Months | Aim | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | Publish the repositories and the archive copy of the specification; appoint a second steward; file the trademark application; first legal advice on the foundation | A stranger can implement the specification from the text and vectors alone |
| 4 to 6 | A second independent implementation; invite comments; open a W3C Community Group | Both implementations pass the same vectors |
| 7 to 9 | A pilot with a small number of real sittings and two or three verifiers; revise to v0.2 | A public pilot report |
| 10 to 12 | Third implementer; foundation formed if two council members and a lawyer are in place; transparency log with a second operator | Governance independent of the founder |

## Roles needed

Second steward; editor; security reviewer; accessibility tester; translators and native-language reviewers; independent implementers; a lawyer in the UAE. All are open; none is filled except the interim steward.

## Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood and effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Key person: one steward | High likelihood, high effect | A second steward; a sealed succession instruction; the sunset clause in the [governance charter](/governance/) |
| Legal entity: the foundation cannot be formed with one person | Certain today | The free-zone foundation regimes examined (ADGM, DIFC) require a council of at least two; other forms to be confirmed by a lawyer; interim holding in trust |
| Trademark: the name is common and may be refused or conflict | Medium | Clearance before filing; the descriptor "Sanad Protocol"; a fall-back name |
| Funding: none | High | Lean costs; volunteers first; no dependence on any single funder |
| Adoption: nobody implements it | Medium to high | Small specification, reference code, vectors, outreach to groups that serve people without portable proof |
| Reputation: confusion with accreditation | Medium | Wording rules in the specification and on this site |
| Security and privacy flaws in the design | Medium | Public review, a security reviewer, a security policy |
