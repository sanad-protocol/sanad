## 7 November 2026: reference implementation and test vectors published

The repository now contains a reference implementation of the recognition function (TypeScript, no dependencies), 15 recognition vectors and 5 inference vectors, and a command-line verifier: `node reference/verify.mjs`. See [Conformance](/conformance/).

</section>

<section id="2026-11-07-spec">

## 7 November 2026: specification v0.1 published

The first version of the Sanad specification (data model, recognition function, transparency log, privacy and threats) is published and open for comment. See the [specification](/spec/0.1/).

</section>
<section id="2026-11-07-name">

## 7 November 2026: name and legal form decided

The protocol is called Sanad. The Open Sanad Foundation is to be established in the United Arab Emirates; until it exists, Dr. Arash Kermani Kolankeh acts as interim steward. See [Governance](/governance/).

</section>
<section id="2026-11-07-site">

## 7 November 2026: website published

sanadprotocol.org is online in English and German. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no external requests.

</section>
