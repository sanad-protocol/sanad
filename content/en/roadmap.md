<!-- glance -->
- Four phases; each ends with a gate that must be met before the next begins.
- Dates are targets, not predictions. Progress is measured by independent implementations, verifiers and attestors.
<!-- /glance -->

<ol class="timeline">
<li><span class="when">Nov to Dec 2026</span><h3>Phase 0: Foundation</h3><p>Freeze the draft v0.1. Neutral organisation, repositories, permanent archive with a DOI, website. Reference implementation, vectors and explainer. Legal checklist (GDPR, UAE law, EU AI Act for matching).</p><p class="gate"><strong>Gate:</strong> the specification can be implemented from the text and the vectors alone.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">Q1 2027</span><h3>Phase 1: Proof</h3><p>A second, independent implementation in another programming language. A pilot of 20 to 30 sittings with three verifiers. W3C Community Group opened. Pilot report; version 0.2.</p><p class="gate"><strong>Gate:</strong> both implementations pass the same vectors; the pilot report is public.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">Q2 to Q3 2027</span><h3>Phase 2: Spread</h3><p>A third independent implementer. A live transparency log with daily checkpoints. Outreach to credential projects and recognition offices. A paper with a DOI. Attestor partners in about five countries. Privacy and security review. Release candidate 1.0.</p><p class="gate"><strong>Gate:</strong> three implementers, ten attestor organisations, three verifiers.</p></li>
<li><span class="when">2028</span><h3>Phase 3: Institution</h3><p>Version 1.0 frozen. A non-profit foundation owns the name, the domain and the log keys. A Working Group charter or an equivalent is proposed.</p><p class="gate"><strong>Gate:</strong> governance independent of the founder; at least three independent stewards from two regions.</p></li>
</ol>

## Measures of progress

Independent implementations that pass the vectors; verifiers that publish a policy; attestor organisations with public records; disputes resolved and recorded. User counts are not a measure.

## Funding

No funding is secured. Public funds for open internet infrastructure change from year to year and some programmes pause; none is assumed. See [Support](/support/).
